## 1. Purpose

V0.8 is the next confirmatory/explanatory experiment after V0.7. V0.7 established that a universal representation policy must **not** be frozen for the tested DeepSeek benchmark. V0.8 therefore does not attempt to rescue or override V0.7.

The purpose of V0.8 is to estimate whether the effect of the `representation` transformation depends jointly on **task and model**.

The central experimental question is:

> Is the effect of `POLICY` heterogeneous across the `TASK Ãƒâ€” MODEL` matrix?

The intended downstream artifact is a **Policy Evidence Matrix** that can later support conditional policy learning. V0.8 itself does not perform online policy learning and does not mutate a production policy during collection.

## 14. Interpretation boundary

V0.8 can establish that policy effects differ or do not differ across the tested `TASK Ãƒâ€” MODEL` matrix.

V0.8 does not, by itself, establish:

- a universal winner;
- a universal token reduction ratio;
- a production routing rule;
- a causal claim beyond the frozen experimental manipulation and protocol;
- cross-provider fungibility of token counts;
- guaranteed future performance on unseen tasks.

A future policy-learning stage must treat V0.8 evidence as training/evidence input and validate learned routing on held-out data.

---

## 15. Freeze boundary

There is **no universal representation freeze criterion in V0.8**.

The primary output is the interaction evidence and the 12-cell Policy Evidence Matrix.

Any future policy freeze must be separately preregistered with:

- held-out validation;
- explicit acceptance criteria;
- fixed risk/benefit thresholds;
- replayability;
- rollback/fallback behavior.

This preserves the PromptForge principle:

> No optimization is better than unsafe optimization.

and, operationally:

> `NOOP` is a first-class policy.
